{% comment %} The venue groups whose positions changed, applied wherever they live by htmx. **The address page's own partial, not a copy of it.** `programgroups` is `{% partialdef %}`-ed in `snippets/dynamic/asset.html` and rendered twice: once when the page is built and once from here, from the snapshot the live pass published. That is the same rule the figure fragments follow next door, and it matters more here than it does there - a group carries a heading, a count, a subtotal that appears only above two positions, a pin control per row and a breakdown panel under it, and a second copy of all that in JavaScript would drift the first time any of it changed, silently, because nothing renders both and compares. `hx-swap-oob` rides along under `oob`, because the attribute has to be on the swapped element and the page must not carry it. The target is the group's own `id="pg-f"`, which the page renders at rest for exactly this. **Whole groups rather than the rows that moved**, because a row arriving changes three things outside itself: the count beside the venue name, the subtotal in the money column, and whether either of them is rendered at all. Sending the row alone leaves a group of two labelled as a group of one. Nothing is sent for an asset whose positions match what the reader already has, so a block that opened one position re-renders one group.
